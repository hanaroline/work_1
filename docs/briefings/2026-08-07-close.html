The close reads as a mild loss, but the index ran from a high of 6,415.60 to a low of 6,158.73 — a 256.87-point range, 4.00% of the high. It opened 1.09% higher, reached 6,415 in the morning, fell to 6,158 in the afternoon, and recovered 100 points into the close. The trigger was SK Hynix, down 4.88% on reports that Nvidia is considering lower HBM specifications for its next-generation “Rubin Ultra” chip, with foreigners selling close to KRW 860bn. Yet on KOSPI 554 stocks rose against 322 that fell: LG Energy Solution +4.35%, Hanwha Aerospace +4.08%, Samsung Electro-Mechanics +3.99%, KB Financial +2.51% — only large-cap semis pushed the index down. On KOSDAQ, by contrast, 829 fell against 808 that rose: broadly, evenly soft. The two markets behaved in opposite ways. The won firmed 7.7 to 1,416.1.
