The number looks like a quiet day; the session was not. With the Nasdaq at a record overnight, the index opened 2.20% higher at 7,161.61 and reached 7,171.44 intraday, then gave back all 153.53 points in the afternoon. Semiconductors did the giving back: SK Hynix, at ₩1,935,000 intraday, closed −1.50%, and Samsung Electronics, up to ₩283,500, ended +0.91%. KOSDAQ turned down, −0.23%. And the won strengthened 22.8 to 1,358.2 per dollar, a 1.65% move — the largest number in today’s edition.

① 간밤 미국은 컸고, 국내는 그것을 개장에 다 썼습니다. 21일(현지) 나스닥이 +2.26% 로 종가 사상 최고, 필라델피아 반도체지수가 +4.29%, 메타가 +11.34%, AMD가 +9.95% 였습니다 — 보도는 메타의 인공지능 에이전트가 앱스토어 무료 1위에 오른 것을 계기로 적었고, AMD는 시가총액 1조 달러를 처음 넘었습니다 1 SOURCE. 코스피는 그 소식을 시가에 2.20% 반영하고 시작했는데, 종가는 +0.15% 입니다. 간밤 재료가 없어서가 아니라, 개장 한 시간에 다 쓰였기 때문입니다.① The overnight move was large, and Korea spent it at the open. On 21 September in New York the Nasdaq closed at a record, +2.26%, with the Philadelphia semiconductor index +4.29%, Meta +11.34% and AMD +9.95% — reporting tied it to Meta’s AI agent reaching number one among free apps, and AMD passed a trillion dollars in market value for the first time 1 SOURCE. KOSPI priced 2.20% of that into the opening print and closed +0.15%. Not for want of news, but because the news was spent in the first hour.

② 그런데 오늘은 폭이 어제보다 나아졌습니다. 코스피에서 오른 종목 383 대 내린 종목 463 입니다 — 여전히 내린 쪽이 많지만, 1.65% 올랐던 어제(310 대 552)보다 고른 하루였습니다. 지수가 덜 오른 날의 시장이 더 넓었던 셈입니다. 거래대금도 20조 8,112억원에서 22조 1,783억원으로 늘었습니다. 다만 코스닥은 반대입니다 — 지수가 −0.23% 이고 오른 종목 625 대 내린 종목 1,002, 하한가가 둘 나왔습니다.② Breadth, though, was better than yesterday. On KOSPI 383 stocks rose against 463 that fell — still more decliners, but a more even day than yesterday’s 1.65% gain on 310 against 552. The market was wider on the day the index moved less. Turnover also rose, from ₩20.8112tn to ₩22.1783tn. KOSDAQ went the other way: the index −0.23%, 625 advancers against 1,002 decliners, and two limit-down names.
