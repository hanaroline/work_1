How to use

What you can do, and how to read the numbers

This tool starts from what an ETF actually holds. Similar names can hold different things, and different names can hold the same things.

1

Browse

Narrow down, then click to look inside.

  1. Type anything — fund name, code, index, or even a holding's name.
  2. Narrow with the eight filters. Reset clears them all.
  3. Click a column header to sort — return, AUM, fee, top-10 weight.
  4. Click a row and it expands in place.
  5. Tick the leftmost “Compare” box to add the fund to the compare tray — see below.
Ticking and clicking are different. The checkbox adds to the compare tray; clicking anywhere else on the row expands the fund's detail.
1-1

The “Compare” column — picking

Pick funds to compare later. The same column appears on both Browse and Rankings, and the tray is shared.

  1. Click the checkbox — the whole cell is clickable, not just the box. With a keyboard: Tab then Space.
  2. Picked rows are tinted with an orange bar on the left, so your picks stay visible as you scroll.
  3. A bar appears above the list showing the count, the names, and buttons to Clear or go to Compare.
  4. With only one picked the Compare button stays disabled — comparing needs at least two.
  5. At 8 the remaining boxes lock and the bar shows “full”. Untick one, or press Clear.
  6. To remove: untick the box, press × on the card in the Compare screen, or Clear the whole tray.
  7. The Rankings screen has the same column. Tick a fund right where you see it in the ranking — no need to go back and search for it. The tray is shared across screens.
Picks survive navigation — changing filters or switching tabs keeps the tray. They are not saved, so reopening the file clears it.
Too few results? Untick “with holdings only”.
1-2

After clicking a row

Three blocks open: key figures, top 10 holdings, period returns.

① Key figures

Manager, index, AUM, fee, price, top-10 weight, yield, premium, tracking error, listing date.

② Top 10 holdings

Bar length is the weight. A dash means the source gave no weight — only the ranking is meaningful.

③ Period returns

Five columns: period (with base date), total return, NAV return, percentile rank in category, gap vs category average.

2

Compare

How much do two funds overlap?

  1. Tick 2–8 funds in the Compare column — on the Browse or the Rankings screen. With only one picked the Compare button stays disabled.
  2. Switch to Compare. Summaries sit side by side, with an overlap table below.
  3. Read the overlap table. Each cell is the shared weight across top-10 holdings.
  4. Then the stock × fund matrix. ○ = held but weight unknown, · = not held.
  5. To drop just one, press the × at the card's top right; the rest stay. Use Clear to empty the tray.
Overlap is a lower bound — only top-10 holdings are compared.
3

Stock → ETF

The reverse: which funds hold this stock?

  1. Type a stock name — Korean or foreign.
  2. Results are ranked by weight — the top ones lean hardest on that stock.
  3. Click a row to open that fund's detail.
Use it to avoid doubling up on a stock you already hold.
4

Rankings

Four rankings: returns, AUM, 3-month flows, TER.

  1. Pick scope and period at the top; all four tables redraw.
  2. Top and bottom returns sit side by side.
  3. Leveraged and inverse funds are off by default — they would dominate the top.
  4. Click a row — detail opens below without pushing the tables.
  5. Tick the “Compare” box here too — it is the same tray as on the Browse screen.
Some funds are excluded from the AUM ranking because the source reports whole-fund assets.

Printing and saving as PDF

Only the screen you are looking at comes out — not all six.

  1. Press “Print this screen / PDF” at the top right. Only the tab that is open at that moment goes to paper.
  2. To keep a PDF, choose “Save as PDF” as the printer in the print dialog.
  3. For another screen, switch to that tab and press it again.
  4. Buttons and the tab bar are dropped from the printout, and tables are kept from splitting across pages.
  5. To keep this guide as a file, use the Download buttons at the top of this screen — the saved file opens on its own, offline.
Printing the browse list uses a lot of paper — it prints every fund matching your filters. Narrow the filters first.

Before you read the numbers

These are the things that actually went wrong while building this tool.

The date beside each period is the base date. Compare it first when another screen shows a different figure. One day of difference can flip a 1-month return when the market moved 10% that day.
One basis only: total return. Distributions are reinvested, so figures run higher than a plain price change — by 20pp or more for high-distribution covered-call funds. 3Y/5Y are annualised.
Only the top 10 holdings. Overlap figures are therefore a lower bound. Where the source gives no weights, only the ranking is meaningful.
Category = market, asset class, region and gearing. Percentile rank: 1 is best, 100 is worst. The average is arithmetic. Geared funds are counted separately; suspended funds are excluded from all rankings.
AUM is ranked only among like measures. For some foreign ETFs the source reports the whole fund's assets across all share classes. Those are excluded from the AUM ranking and labelled in the detail view.
A few funds are missing distributions. Where the source has no distribution history, total return equals price return and understates by roughly the yield — mostly bond funds, 1–3.6pp. Such funds are flagged in the detail view.
A few funds use a different source for returns. Returns are normally computed here from Yahoo daily bars. For a few Korean funds the source has no daily history at all, so Naver's own price return is carried over instead and labelled as such in the detail view. Being a price return from a different source, it is excluded from the list and ranking columns.

Where the data comes from

Returns as of 2026-10-14 · 1,228 of 1,360 funds carry this date; the rest had no trade that day. Korean ETFs from Naver Finance. Overseas ETFs from Yahoo Finance.

Weights come from the prior business day's portfolio deposit file, not live holdings. Every figure passes a full audit before publication; if the audit finds an error the data is not updated.

Can't find a fund?

Untick "with holdings only" — funds whose source provides no holdings are hidden by default. Leveraged and inverse funds are also off by default.

Otherwise it is outside this tool's universe: all Korean listings, plus major foreign listings only.
